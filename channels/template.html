__TITLE__

Each chart shows three years of FMP daily closes. A straight-line regression runs from each stock's 3-year low to the latest close (__ASOF_TXT__), with bands at ±2 standard deviations of the residuals. A blue channel runs from the lowest close of the past 2 years and a teal one from the lowest close of the past 12 months. The pills show where today's price sits in each channel, in σ. Grey horizontal lines mark support: price levels below today's close where the stock bottomed at least twice (swing lows within 3% of each other), up to three per chart, labelled with the level and number of touches. Red lines are earlier supports the price has since fallen through (closed more than 2% below after the last touch); the ✕ marks the break, and they often act as resistance on the way back up.
